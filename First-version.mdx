### **It all starts with a prompt.**

Think about the app or website that you want to build and describe it in detail by answering these questions: 

1. **Basic details**: Do you see your future product as an app or a website? Or is it perhaps a widget?
2. **Capabilities**: What should your app do? How will you or your audience interact with it? 
3. **Design**: What does your app look like? Should it use bright colours and emojis, or maybe even a specific color palette, or should it be more professional and minimalistic? 
4. **Context**: Do you have any references that provide additional context? Share them with Kineto so it understands better what you need. 
5. **Special features**: Do you want your app to have any special features, such as integration with Google Maps or Google Docs? \
   \
   After Kineto will propose you **the Style of your app** , choose one or ask Kineto to generate more options.\
   \
   When the style is settled - its time to **choose a Color Pallete**. You can use one of generated by Kineto or use your own palette to make app as you imagined it.\
   \
   Next step - **Brainstorm on the Features**. Kineto will advise you what features your app will have. Feel free to edit, delete or add more features to your app.\
   \
   When you finished - tap **Let's create it** and Kineto will show you a concept of your app. If everything is fine - tap \*\*Generate full app \*\*and get ready to use your new app!\
   \
   Also you may **attach graphic files** to build in your app, **tap** **the paperclip icon** in left corner of chat to add them.

### **Creating your app may take time**.

Please keep in mind that **app generation may take some time**, depending on the complexity of it. Usually it takes **up to 30 minutes** to be done. Kineto will send you a **notification on email** as it finishes.
