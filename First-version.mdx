### **It all starts with a prompt**

Think about the app or website that you want to build and describe it in detail by answering these questions: 

1. **Basic details**: Do you see your future product as an app or  a website? Or perhaps a widget?
2. **Capabilities**: What should your app do? How will you or your audience interact with it? 
3. **Design**: What does your app look like? Should it use bright colors and emojis, a specific color palette, or a more professional, minimalist design?  
4. **Context**: Do you have any references that could provide additional context? Share a link to them with Kineto to help it understand what you need better.
5. **Special features**: Do you want your app to have any special features, such as integration with Google Maps or Google Docs? \
   \
   After Kineto proposes a style for your app, choose one of the styles or ask it to generate more options.\
   \
   When the style is settled, it's time to **choose a Color Palette**. You can use one of generated by Kineto or use your own palette to make app as you imagined it.\
   \
   The next step is **Brainstorm on the Features**. Kineto will advise you what features your app should have. Feel free to edit, delete or add more features.\
   \
   If you want to attach graphic files to integrate them into your app, tap the paperclip icon in the left corner of the chat window and add these files.\
   \
   When you're done, tap **Let's create it**, and Kineto will show you a concept of your app. If you like what you see, tap **Generate full app**!

### **Creating your product may take time**

Please keep in mind that generation takes some time, depending on the complexity of your app or website. It usually takes **up to 30 minutes**. Kineto will send you an email notification once it is finished.
