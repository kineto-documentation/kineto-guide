title: "Kinetik MCP Connector for ChatGPT"
---

Kinetik for ChatGPT creates social media content in your brand straight from a ChatGPT conversation: images, carousels, short videos and media projects of several posts for one goal. You see the results in an interactive view inside the chat. Kinetik keeps everything you make, so you can find it again from any chat.

## What you can do

| Tool | What it does | Changes anything? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Research trends and news** | Finds trending posts and recent news in your niche to base new content on. | No (read-only) |
| **Create content** | Makes an image, a carousel, a short video or a media project of several pieces, in your brand. | Saves the new content to your Kinetik projects |
| **Edit content** | Changes content already made: restyle it, adjust a slide, shorten a caption, recut a video. | Saves a new version |
| **Find content** | Finds what you have made, by a question in your own words. | No (read-only) |
| **Analyze an account** | Analyzes a social account's content and results, and suggests what to post next. | No (read-only) |
| **Publish content** | Publishes a finished piece to your connected social account: an Instagram image, carousel or reel, a TikTok video as a draft, or a LinkedIn text post. | **Yes.** |
| **Add your files** | Adds the photos, videos or documents you attach to your message, so they can be used in new content. | Stores the files in your Kinetik account |
| **Check a task** | Checks the status of a task and returns its result. Creating content takes a few minutes. | No (read-only) |
| **Show results** | Shows your content, a media project, the trending effects or a connection card in an interactive view. | No (read-only) |
| **Morning wave** | Sets up and runs a daily brief of 3 to 5 fresh post ideas for your niche. | Run: saves the day's ideas |

Try asking:

- "Show me what's trending" (then pick an effect and attach your photo)
- "Make an Instagram carousel about our new cold-brew kit"
- "Find the carousels I made last week"
- "Analyze @myaccount and suggest three posts"
- "Publish this carousel to Instagram"

## How to connect

Kinetik is a ChatGPT plugin. Add it on ChatGPT on the web; it then works in your chats.

1. In ChatGPT, open [**Plugins**](https://chatgpt.com/plugins), search for **Kinetik** and install it.
2. Click **Connect** and sign in to your Kineto account.
3. Review and approve the access requested.
4. In a chat, pick **Kinetik** with `@` or just ask, for example "show me what's trending".

**Adding it by URL** (before Kinetik appears in the directory, or for testing):

1. Open [**Plugins**](https://chatgpt.com/plugins), click **\+** and choose **Add custom MCP server**.
2. Enter the name **Kinetik**, and under **Connection** the server URL `https://kineto.app/api/charms/mcp-kinetik`.
3. Choose **OAuth** for authentication and leave the client ID and secret empty: Kinetik registers ChatGPT automatically.
4. Read the warning, click **I understand and want to continue**, then **Create as a plugin**.
5. Install the plugin, then sign in to Kineto when asked.

On a Business or Enterprise workspace, an admin may have to allow custom MCP servers first.

To publish or analyze your own account, connect it when Kinetik asks. Kinetik shows a connection card in the chat. Instagram needs a Business or Creator account, and its sign-in page is provided by Composio.

## What data is shared

When you use Kinetik in ChatGPT, Kineto receives:

- the requests ChatGPT sends to Kinetik's tools, such as your brief, the platforms and the style;
- the files you attach for a post;
- the access you approved and the authorisation tokens.

Kinetik sends back what it made, its status and links to your files. Links open only for you, signed in to Kineto.

Kinetik does not receive your other ChatGPT conversations, files or memory. It sees only what is sent to its tools.

Content is made by Kinetik's AI model providers and stored in your Kineto account, as described in the [Privacy Notice](https://kineto.app/legal/privacy-notice). Nothing is posted to a social account unless you confirm it. OpenAI processes the data it receives under its own privacy policy.

## How to disconnect

- **In Kineto:** Settings → Personal → Connectors → Disconnect.
- **In ChatGPT:** open [Plugins](https://chatgpt.com/plugins) and remove or uninstall Kinetik.

Content you made stays in your Kineto account until you delete it.
