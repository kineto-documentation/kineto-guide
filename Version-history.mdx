---
title: "Version History"
description: "How to operate the versions of the app"
---

To review all versions of your app or website, open the **Version History** tab in the top right corner of the chat. You can browse past versions and restore any of them. This feature is useful if you're unhappy with the current state of the product and want to revert to an earlier version to continue editing.\
\
Keep in mind that if you return to an older version and make changes, your most recent version will be overwritten!
