---
title: "Version History"
description: "How to operate with versions of the app"
---

To view all versions of your app or website, open the **Version History** tab in right up corner of the chat. You can browse past versions and restore any of them — useful if you’re unhappy with the current state and want to roll back a few steps to continue editing from an earlier point.\
\
Keep in mind that if you **return to old version and make changes** - your **old 'newest" version** **will be overwritten**.
