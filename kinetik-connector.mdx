---
title: "Kinetik MCP Connector"
---

The Kinetik connector lets Claude use your Kinetik skills. They run in your own private cloud sandbox, which keeps your files and work between chats. With them you can create images, carousels and videos, research what's trending, and publish to your connected social accounts. Results are shown in an interactive view in the chat.

<Note>
  Using ChatGPT? See [Kinetik MCP Connector for ChatGPT](/kinetik-mcp-connector-for-chat-gpt).
</Note>

## What you can do

| Tool | What it does | Changes anything? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Find the user's skills** / **Load a skill** | Lists your installed Kinetik skills and loads one's instructions. | No (read-only) |
| **Find a skill the user could install** | Searches skills you don't have yet. Installing one is up to you. | No (read-only) |
| **Run a command in the Charms sandbox** | Runs a skill's command in your private cloud sandbox, for example to make a carousel. | Yes: creates or changes files in your sandbox |
| **Check / list / cancel a background job** | Follows longer work, such as a video, until it is done, or stops it. | Cancel stops a running job |
| **Read / list files** | Reads and lists files in your sandbox. | No (read-only) |
| **Write / edit / delete a file** | Changes text files in your sandbox, or deletes a file. | Yes: in your sandbox only |
| **Send a file to the Charms sandbox** | Puts a file you give it, such as a photo or a PDF, into your sandbox. | Yes: in your sandbox only |
| **Show the user a rendered view** | Shows images, galleries, videos, media projects, upload forms and connection cards. | No (read-only) |

Publishing to a social account is done by a skill and needs that account to be connected first. Kinetik asks for the connection with a card in the chat.

## How to connect

1. In Claude, open **Settings → Connectors**, search the directory for **Kinetik** and click **Add**. Or choose **Add custom connector** and enter `https://kineto.app/api/agents/mcp`.
2. Click **Connect** and sign in to your Kineto account.
3. Review and approve the access requested.

## What data is shared

When you use the connector, Kineto receives:

- the requests Claude sends to Kinetik's tools, such as commands for your skills;
- the files you send to your sandbox;
- the access you approved and the authorisation tokens.

Kinetik sends back the results, your files and links to them. Links open only for you, signed in to Kineto.

Kinetik does not receive your other Claude conversations, files or memory. It sees only what is sent to its tools.

Your sandbox and the content in it are stored in your Kineto account, as described in the [Privacy Notice](https://kineto.app/legal/privacy-notice). Anthropic processes the data it receives under its own privacy policy.

## How to disconnect

- **In Kineto:** Settings → Personal → Connectors → Disconnect.
- **In Claude:** remove the Kinetik connector from your connectors.
