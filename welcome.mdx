Welcome to Kineto, the AI-powered platform designed to help you easily create websites and web applications. No code, just chat.

Whether you're building a personal project or a professional app, Kineto will bring your ideas to life.

### How it works

- **Describe your app:** Just tell Kineto what you want your app to do, and it will handle the rest.
- **Generate and preview:** Watch your app being created and preview its functionality. See if there's anything you want to change.
- **Edit and refine:** Fine-tune your app's features, design, and style until it's exactly as you imagined. You can do this either via the chat or manually on the meta-layer by dragging and changing the layout, images, and text.
- **Enjoy your app:** Once you're happy with it, the product will be deployed and then ready for use. Share it with friends and use it for work or personal projects!

### What you can create with Kineto

Kineto works best when asked to build apps with single-value (or mostly single-value) functionality. Within that scope, it can build just  anything you can think of:

- Educational or entertainment websites or apps, such as quizzes, casual games, etc.
- Websites for your business, portfolio, or side projects.
- Personal or professional apps and dashboards.
