---
title: "Troubleshooting"
description: "How to deal with problems"
---

**Kineto misunderstood my request**\
Try explaining in more detail or uploading a screenshot for reference. If the results still aren’t what you expect, you can always ask for help on [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/WZqyuHtVQa).

**Kineto is stuck during code generation**\
Click **Stop** and resend your request. If that doesn’t work, report the bug with a link and screenshot on [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/WZqyuHtVQa).

**There’s an error**\
Click **Try again**. If there’s no option to do so, contact support on [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/WZqyuHtVQa).

**A recent change broke the project**\
Go to **View history** (the icon above the chat), select a previous version that worked well, and click **Restore**. But keep in mind that when you will edit previous version, your current one will disappear.

**The preview screen is gray, but no error appears**\
This usually means something went wrong during the final project build. Ask Kineto to make a small change – that often fixes the issue. If it doesn’t, ask for help on [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/WZqyuHtVQa).
