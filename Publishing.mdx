---
title: "Publishing "
description: "How to publish your app as it ready."
---

After you’ve generated and edited your app, it’s time to share it! To do this, click the **Publish** button in the top-right corner. You’ll get a link to your app that you can share with others.\
\
Now you have 2 versions of your app - preview or dev and published one.\
\
If you’ve edited your dev app afterward, you need to update the published version.\
Dev and puvlished versions have different databases, so for example, if its a game, the results on dev version wont be seen on published one. \
\
Also, if you have users registered in your published version - usually you may republish it after changes without loosing users data, so don't worry about your users, their data is safe.
