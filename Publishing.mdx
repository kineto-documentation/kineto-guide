Once you've generated and edited your app, it's time to share it! Click the **Publish** button in the top-right corner to do this. You'll receive a link to your app that you can share with others.\
\
You now have two versions of your app: a **preview** version and a **published** one.\
\
If you've edited your preview app afterwards, you need to update the published version.\
\
Preview and published versions have different databases. For instance, if you've created a game, the results on the dev version won't be seen in the published app until you update it. \
\
Also, if you have users registered in your published version, you can republish it after making changes. Rest assured that your users' data is safe.
