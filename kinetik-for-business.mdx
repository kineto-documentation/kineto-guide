**Kinetik for Teams**

_Kinetik is an AI agent for social-first marketing teams. It handles trend research, content production, analytics, and influencer operations — inside the tools you already use._

**How it works**

Kinetik runs as a teammate in Slack (or any messenger your team uses). You talk to it in DMs or invite it to group channels. It has persistent memory — every conversation builds on the previous one, so you don't repeat context.

**What makes it different from a chatbot:**

|  | **Generic AI chatbot** | **Kinetik** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Where it lives** | Separate tab or app | Your existing Slack workspace |
| **Memory** | Resets each session | Remembers your team, projects, brand voice, and past conversations |
| **Work style** | Responds when asked | Proactively follows up on tasks, sends scheduled reports, flags issues |
| **Output** | Text in a chat window | Reports with sources and links, drafted posts, influencer longlists, analytics summaries |
| **Context** | You explain everything from scratch | Connected to your analytics, content, and workflows |

**What Kinetik does**

**Trend & market research**

Monitors competitors, market shifts, and trending topics across X, Instagram, TikTok, and news sources. Delivers structured reports to Slack — daily, weekly, or on demand.

**Example:**

_"Morning! 3 things to know about your market today:_\
 _• Rhode just teased a launch — X is buzzing. 18K mentions in 2 hours._\
 _• "Undone hair" is the #1 TikTok beauty trend right now — fits your audience._\
 _• Competitor X posted a carousel that got 4× their average engagement. Here's what they did differently."_

**Content creation & SEO**

Writes social posts, blog drafts, and scripts in your brand voice. Adapts one piece of content for multiple platforms — LinkedIn, X, Instagram, blog. Generates visuals. Sticks to your tone guidelines.

**Example:**

**You:** We're launching new sneakers Friday. Need content asap for Insta and X.\
 **Kinetik:** Done. Reels script, carousel draft, and teaser caption attached. I also drafted a TikTok version — unscripted format, should work well for your audience.

**Campaign & product analytics**

Pulls data from GA4, Amplitude, Meta Ads, and other connected sources. Highlights what's working, flags what's not. Delivered as a Slack summary — weekly or on demand.

**Example:**

_"The two dashboards use different event definitions for "new user."_\
 _• Product Dashboard uses backend\_users\_started, source: link_\
 _• Marketing Dashboard uses backend\_users\_started \+ messenger filter_\
\
 _This explains the 15% discrepancy in your reports."_

**Influencer operations**

Sources creators that match your ICP, scores them on reach and relevance, drafts outreach emails. Structured in tiers for easy decision-making.

**Example:**

**You:** Prepare a longlist of influencers on YouTube and X for our product. Evaluate relevance, reach, and engagement. Brief attached.\
 **Kinetik:** Longlist ready, structured in 3 tiers:\
 • Tier 1 — High-reach creators with proven brand alignment\
 • Tier 2 — Mid-size creators actively discussed in the community\
 • Tier 3 — Rising creators with strong engagement growth

**Project management**

Tracks deadlines in Slack, sends reminders, proposes drafts, and flags blockers before they become problems.

**Example:**

**You:** We're preparing for a B2B event. Read the doc with deadlines, set up alerts, and estimate time needed per task.\
 **Kinetik:** Reviewed all deadlines:\
 • Submission needs 1 working day. I'll prepare the draft. Alert set 2 days before.\
 • Booth design needs at least 3 weeks. Step-by-step plan shared 3 weeks before.\
 • Final review and speaker prep needs 5 working days. Alert set.
