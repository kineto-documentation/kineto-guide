### **It all starts with a prompt**

Think about the app or website that you want to build and describe it in detail by answering these questions: 

5. **Basic details:** Do you see your future product as an app or a website? Or perhaps a widget?
5. **Capabilities:** What should your app do? How will you or your audience interact with it?
5. **Design:** What will your app look like? Should it use bright colors and emojis, a specific color palette, or a more professional, minimalist design?
5. **Context:** Do you have any references that provide additional context? Share them with Kineto so it can better understand what you need.
5. **Special features:** Would you like your app to have special features, such as integration with Google Maps or Google Docs?  \

### **Creating your app may take time**

Depending on the complexity of your project, generating an app with Kineto may require up to 30 minutes. Don’t worry, Kineto will send you an email when your app is ready.
