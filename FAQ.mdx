**Do I need coding to create with Kineto**\
\
No, you don’t need to know how to code — with Kineto, anyone can create the app they need. Just ask Kineto and describe what you’d like to build.\
**How should i promt?**\
\
Think about the app or website that you want to build and describe it in detail by answering these questions: 

1. **Basic details**: Do you see your future product as an app or a website?
2. **Capabilities**: What should your app do? How will you or your audience interact with it? 
3. **Design**: What does your app look like? Should it use bright colours and emojis, or maybe even a specific color palette, or should it be more professional and minimalistic? 
4. **Context**: Do you have any references that provide additional context? Share them with Kineto so it understands better what you need. 
5. **Special features**: Do you want your app to have any special features, such as integration with Google Maps or Google Docs? 

\
\
**How do I design my app?**\
Describe in detail in the prompt what your app should be like — include all the design details you consider important. Then, choose one of the suggested design options and color palettes. Before generating the app, Kineto will show you a draft of your application.\
You can always add something or correct after the generation.\
\
**How long does it takes for the code to be generated?**\
Usually it takes up to 20 minutes, depending on the complexity of your app.\
\
**How do I publish and invite users?**\
After you’ve generated and edited your app, it’s time to share it! To do this, click the **Publish** button in the top-right corner. You’ll get a link to your app that you can share with others.\
\
**Can I use images and videos?**

Yes, it’s easy to upload images to use as content on the page and it’s backgrounds, or you can ask Kineto to generate them. \
\
**Can I edit or get the project’s source code?**\
\
No, that’s not necessary.\
Kineto is a turnkey service — you don’t need to edit any code. Once you’ve created the app, you can start using it right away. In the future, Kineto will offer the option to connect your own domain.

**How can I set payments into my app?**\
\
There’s no such option at the moment, but we plan to implement this feature in the future.
