description: "Additional features that you can add to your app"
---

Kineto can build apps that:

- Collect users' emails via poll
- Have integrations with LLM
- Have a role management system with admin or non-admin roles
- Use your graphic files in your app

More new features and integrations are coming soon. To make sure you don't miss anything, [**join our community on Discord**](https://discord.com/invite/WZqyuHtVQa).
