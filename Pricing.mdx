\

**How many credits do I need?**

Credits are Kinetik's energy. Every time you ask it to do something, it gets to work — and the heavier the lift, the more credits it takes.

Light tasks (quick Q&A, caption rewrite, hashtag suggestion, reminder): single-digit credits.

Medium tasks (news digest, post drafting with research, image generation): approximately 20–30 credits.

Heavy tasks (deep competitor analysis, video generation, full content plan): 50\+ credits.
