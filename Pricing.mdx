> **New plans from 10 October 2026.** If you subscribed before 10 October 2026, your plan stays the same — see [Plans for subscribers before 10 October 2026](#plans-for-subscribers-before-10-october-2026).

**Free trial** — new users get 70 credits once, enough for up to 35 slides or about 17 seconds of video. No card or commitment needed.

**Starter** — 140 credits: up to 70 slides or 35 sec of video. Standard templates.

**Pro** — 441 credits: 220 slides or 110 sec of video. Viral & animation effects. GPT Image 2.5, Nano Banana 2 & Pro, ElevenLabs.

**Max** — 1,540 credits: 770 slides or 6 min of video. Viral & animation effects. GPT Image 2.5, Nano Banana 2 & Pro, ElevenLabs.

**Standard templates** — designer-made slide layouts. Each one has a clean grid, bold typography, one color palette, and a fixed place for every element: headline, subtitle, topic tag, your handle, and slide number. There are separate layouts for covers and text slides — text-only, full-bleed photo, cut-out photo, half-and-half, framed photo, collage — so every carousel looks consistent and professionally designed, with no design work on your side. Standard templates are static; Pro and Max add viral & animation effects.

**Standard models** — every plan runs on Claude Haiku 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, and Opus 5.5. Pro and Max add GPT Image 2.5, Nano Banana 2 & Pro, ElevenLabs, and other models.

**Trending effects from the feed** — on Pro and Max, Kinetik searches the feed for effects that are trending right now and makes them available for your videos.

## How credits are spent

### Content

| What | Credits |
| :-- | :-- |
| Carousel slide (generation, edit, or regeneration) | 2 |
| Carousel of 10 slides | 20 |
| Video, 1 second | 4 |
| Animated live photo, 3 seconds | 28 |

Editing or regenerating a slide costs the same as creating it.

You can spend your credits on any mix of content. For example, Pro is enough for 10 carousels of 10 slides plus about 60 seconds of video.

### Other jobs

Tasks that aren't slides or video are priced by the work involved:

- **Light** (single-digit credits): hashtag research, reminders, quick web lookups
- **Medium** (tens of credits): news digests, drafting a post with research, static content generation
- **Heavy** (dozens of credits): deep competitor analysis, full content plans with trend research, app generation, video generation

## Included in all plans

- **Integrations:** Instagram, Google Search, Perplexity Search, TikTok, [X.com](http://X.com), Notion, Google Workspace
- **Messengers:** WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack
- **Other capabilities:** voice, image generation, video generation, scheduled tasks, app generation

## Changing your plan

You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Upgrades apply immediately, and new credits are added on top of your balance. Downgrades take effect on your next billing date.

## Plans for subscribers before 10 October 2026

If you subscribed before 10 October 2026, your plan works exactly as before — same price, same monthly credits, and credits are spent the same way as before. These plans are no longer available to new subscribers.

| Plan | Price | Credits / month | Approx. messages |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Creator | \$25 / month | 700 | ≈ 200 |
| Inventor | \$97 / month | 3,500 | ≈ 1,000 |

Prices exclude VAT.

On these plans, credits are spent based on the work involved.

**Switching to a new plan:** you can move to Starter, Pro or Max at any time, but you can't go back to Creator or Inventor afterwards. The same applies if you cancel — once your subscription ends, these plans are no longer available.
